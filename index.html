01 — Company story

Two decades on one discipline: the floor.

Founded in 2005 and headquartered in Farwaniyah, EBACO has focused on a single specialism — floor covering.

From warm laminate to hygienic vinyl, our range is selected to answer the practical demands of Kuwaiti interiors and outdoor spaces.
